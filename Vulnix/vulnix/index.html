03 Execution Matrix

The auditor's toolkit

Read-only commands you can run in a terminal to verify each control. Click copy and paste.

Why commands? Settings screens can hide things. A command shows the real state of your system and gives you proof (the output) to paste into your report. Windows and macOS use different commands, so switch the tab.
04 Categorizing Risk

The CVSS Funnel

Click a Tier to see what Lands in it. CVSS 4.0 Weighs Intrinsic Severity, the Current Exploit Landscape and Your Organizational Context.

Why CVSS? Not Every Flaw is equally Dangerous. CVSS gives each Flaw a score from 0 to 10 so you Fix the Dangerous ones first. You never calculate it by hand. Just remember: 9 and above is fix today, 7 to 8.9 is fix this week, below that can wait.

05 Live Audit

Audit your Own Machine

The Python Backend Runs Read-only checks for Passwords, Firewall, Encryption, Admin Rights, Updates, Guest Account, Antivirus, UAC and Remote Access. If the Server is not Running, the Manual Checklist Below Still Works.

What Happens Here? The Scan only Reads your Settings and Changes Nothing. Every Problem is Marked Red, Orange or Yellow with a HOW-TO-FIX box. Your Password Policy is Scanned, but MFA Lives on your Online Accounts (Google, Microsoft), so Tick that in the Manual Checklist.

07 PROFESSIONAL BENCHMARKS

The Professional Standard

Startups and large corporations use legally binding versions of this exact checklist to prove their security posture to global governments.

What and Why? ISO 27001, SOC 2, and CIS are official rulebooks that companies follow to prove they are secure. Your small checklist is the same idea at a small scale (or foundational level), so what you do here is what professional auditors do at a company scale. It shows employers you understand real standards

ISO 27001:2022

Configuration Management & Monitoring Activities.
Managing secure configurations and monitoring system activities.

SOC 2

Access Controls & System Operations.
Ensuring only authorized access and maintaining system patching.

CIS Controls v8

Asset Inventory & Control.
Knowing exactly what hardware and software exists on your network.
